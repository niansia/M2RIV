# Pre-v0.1 spoken-name and brand clearance study

This protocol operationalizes RFC 0008. It does not grant permission to set
`M2RIV_BRAND_CLEARED` and is not legal advice.

## Participants

Recruit 10-20 people who have not seen the repository. Include at least five
software/ML engineers and five technically literate non-maintainers. Do not
coach spelling or show the logo before recall is measured.

## Procedure

1. Say once: “The project is called em-two-riv.”
2. Ask the participant to repeat it immediately.
3. Discuss an unrelated topic for five minutes.
4. Ask them to type the repository name and say it aloud.
5. Show `M2RIV`; ask how they would pronounce it without prior instruction.
6. Record exact typed answer, spoken answer, hesitation, and any association.

## Decision thresholds

- unaided five-minute spelling at least 80%;
- unaided pronunciation agreement at least 80%;
- no repeated harmful/confusing association;
- GitHub, PyPI, package, domain, and professional trademark review complete;
- at least three cleared alternatives evaluated under the same protocol.

Failing recall is evidence to rename before ecosystem adoption. Passing recall
does not replace legal clearance. Raw participant data must be stored outside the
repository unless explicit consent permits publication.
